# SwaLoop — Hackathon Live Presentation Script

Follow this exact 12-step script to demonstrate the complete verified loop to evaluators within 3–4 minutes:

---

### Step 1: Open the Product Home (`/`)
- Point out the institutional brand, the defining tagline: **"Every waste event. One verified loop."**
- Walk through the 6-stage operational framework: `DETECT → CLASSIFY → VERIFY → ACT → PROVE → MEASURE`.
- Emphasize the core differentiator: *"SwaLoop is not another smart dustbin. It is an operational governance platform."*

---

### Step 2: Open the Operations Dashboard (`/dashboard`)
- Show the clean linear-inspired interface (calm slate palette, compact KPI strip).
- Point out **1,284 Events**, **87.4% Segregation Quality**, and **93.2% Collection Compliance**.
- Point to **"REQUIRES ATTENTION"**: Show `WS-004` (Block A Cafeteria) at **92% capacity overflow risk** with medium food contamination.

---

### Step 3: Open the AI Classifier Workstation (`/classifier`)
- Select the pre-configured benchmark sample: **"Soiled Meal Packaging (Contaminated)"**.
- Watch Gemini AI classify:
  - Stream: **Dry Waste**
  - Confidence: **94% (High)**
  - Contamination Alert: **Medium Severity (Curry gravy residue on cardboard wrapper)**
  - Required Directive: **"Remove food-contaminated packaging before recycling baling."**
- Click **"Confirm & Create Waste Event Passport"**.

---

### Step 4: The Waste Event Passport

- Highlight the **vertical operational timeline**:
  - `10:32 AM` Waste reported

  - `10:34 AM` Contamination detected

  - `10:51 AM` Worker started task

---

### Step 5: Switch to Sanitation Worker Role

  - Tap **"SCAN / ENTER STATION QR"** — validate `SWALOOP-QR-WS-004`.
  - Tap **"MARK COLLECTION COMPLETE"** with photo proof attached.

---
